a bit about me

I've worn many hats in my life...

Performing Arts

...and I'm still discovering and experimenting with how all these aspects of myself coalesce

Yet the thread that weaves through it all is relationship- how to get in right relationship with ourselves, with each other, with our more than human relatives, and with the land and Earth.

I believe there's an ineffable human spirit inside all of us that is full of fire, play, curiosity, and care... that aches to put its creativity towards the affirmation of life.

My goal is to bring that spirit out in people so we can make incredible things together, and hopefully, make the world a better place in the process

If that sounds like you too, let's chat! We just might be future collaborators...
